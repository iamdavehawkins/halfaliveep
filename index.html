about the ep

Half Alive is a five-song collection about finding presence in a world that keeps pulling you away. Written and recorded between quiet mornings and late nights at home, the EP traces the tension between connection and distraction, the loops we get stuck in, the systems we buy into, and the moments that remind us we're still human.
